{% raw %}## maton youtube playlist remove-video

```
maton youtube playlist remove-video <playlistItemId> [flags]
```

Remove a video from a playlist. The argument is the playlistItem ID
(not the video ID) — find it in the ITEM_ID column of
'maton youtube playlist items <playlistId>'.
